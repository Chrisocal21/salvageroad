SALVAGE ROAD - prototype vehicle GLB pack

Includes:
- delivery_step_van.glb : weathered box-style delivery truck
- unfinished_pickup.glb : older pickup, incomplete front and missing bed

These are ORIGINAL PROCEDURAL LOW-POLY BLOCKOUT / PROTOTYPE MODELS, not exact replicas and not production-quality scanned assets.
GLB contains 3D geometry and per-vertex colors; no external textures needed.
Units: meters (approximate). X: vehicle length (front is negative X); Y: left/right; Z: up.
Meshes are divided into named vehicle parts; geometry is not rigged. Wheels are separate geometry but not animation-ready pivots.
Recommend refining topology, creating PBR UV materials, adding detailed interiors, accurate wheel-well openings, optimized LODs, collision meshes and wheel pivots before shipping.
Made for Salvage Road prototype, November 2026.
